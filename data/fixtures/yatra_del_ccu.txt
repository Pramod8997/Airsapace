**Must-Visit Places in Kolkata**

Kolkata is rich in history and culture, with numerous attractions to explore. Here are some must-visit places:

1.   **Victoria Memorial:** An iconic symbol of Kolkata, this grand marble structure is surrounded by beautiful gardens.
2.   **Howrah Bridge:** A marvel of engineering, this bridge connects the city and is an iconic landmark.
3.   **Indian Museum:** The largest and oldest museum in India, showcasing a vast collection of artifacts.
4.   **Marble Palace:** A stunning private mansion known for its art collection and architecture.
5.   **Dakshineswar Kali Temple:** A revered temple located on the banks of the Hooghly River.

Make sure to plan your visits through yatra for convenience.

**Delicious Foods to Try in Kolkata**

Kolkata is a food lover's paradise, offering a variety of delicious dishes that reflect its rich culinary heritage. Don't miss the following foods:

**Biriyani:** A fragrant rice dish, often made with chicken or mutton, served with raita.

**Fish Curry:** Bengali-style fish curry, typically made with mustard oil and spices.

**Puchka (Pani Puri):** A popular street snack filled with tangy water and spices.

**Sweets:** Savor traditional sweets like Rasgulla and Sandesh.

**Street Food:** Explore the vibrant street food scene in areas like Park Street and Gariahat.

For food tours and culinary experiences, check out yatra for options.

**Fun Things to Do in Kolkata**

Kolkata offers a plethora of fun activities for all ages. Here are some suggestions:

1.   **Visit the Science City:** An interactive science museum perfect for families.
2.   **Take a Boat Ride on the Hooghly:** Enjoy picturesque views of the city from the water.
3.   **Explore Eco Park:** A vast park with recreational activities and beautiful landscapes.
4.   **Attend a Cultural Show:** Experience the local culture through dance and music performances.
5.   **Shopping at New Market:** A bustling market offering everything from clothes to handicrafts.
